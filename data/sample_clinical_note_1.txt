Clinical Review Note
Patient Review Summary:
The patient was reviewed following a recent period of clinical deterioration. Concerns were raised regarding a decline in functional status and overall mobility.
Findings:
There is clear evidence of reduced mobility compared to baseline, with the patient requiring increased assistance for ambulation. This has contributed to a heightened risk of falls, particularly during unsupervised periods.
Additionally, the current care plan appears insufficient in addressing night-time supervision needs, which may further increase safety risks.
Assessment:
The patient’s condition suggests a need for reassessment of both mobility support and supervision strategies. The existing care arrangements may not adequately mitigate risks associated with reduced mobility and potential falls, especially overnight.
Recommendations:
•	It would be beneficial for the multidisciplinary team to consider referral for physiotherapy assessment, with the aim of improving mobility and reducing fall risk. 
•	A review of the night-time care plan is recommended to ensure appropriate supervision and safety measures are in place. 
•	Environmental and assistive interventions (e.g., mobility aids, fall prevention strategies) should also be evaluated. 
Plan / Actions:
•	Arrange physiotherapy review at the earliest opportunity. 
•	Update care plan to include enhanced night-time supervision if required. 
•	Monitor mobility status and reassess fall risk regularly. 
Follow-Up:
The patient should be reviewed within two weeks to assess progress, response to interventions, and any further deterioration or improvement.
